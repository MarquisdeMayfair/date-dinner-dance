Privacy

Date · Dinner · Dance is a public night planner. You spin a place to go, a place to eat, and a dancefloor, then share the itinerary. It is not a dating app, not a matchmaker, and not a drinks shop. “Date” here means a day out.

What we do not do

  • No user profiles, likes, matches, or in-app chat.
  • No Facebook Login or Instagram Login for the planner.
  • No selling of drinks, tickets, or table bookings.
  • No tracking people across other sites for ads.

What we collect

The planner works without an account. Venue pages, photos, and Instagram links are public listings.

If you tap Notify me, we keep the email (and optional Instagram handle) you type so we can tell you when a new city goes live. That list is not sold.

If you open or share a night, we count the city and the three places, and which share button you used. We do this so we know which nights people actually pick, even when nobody signs up. We drop known bots and crawlers. We do not store your name, and we do not post to Instagram for you.

Write to us at hello@datedinnerdance.com.
